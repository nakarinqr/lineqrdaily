# LineQrDaily Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

LineQrDaily is a personal desktop tool used only by its owner (nakarinqrline@gmail.com) on their own computer.

## What data the app accesses

With the owner's permission through Google sign-in, the app uses the Google Photos Library API to upload images and create albums in the owner's own Google Photos account. It only works with content that the app itself creates.

## How data is stored

Images and the Google sign-in token are kept only on the owner's computer and in the owner's Google account. The app has no server and does not send data anywhere else.

## Sharing

The app does not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties, and does not use it for advertising.

LineQrDaily's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

## Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions
